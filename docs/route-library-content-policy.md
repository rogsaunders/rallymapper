# Route Library — Content Policy, Safety Disclaimer & Author Terms (DRAFT)

**Status:** First draft, 2026-06-22. **Not legal advice.** This scaffolds the
trust/safety layer the Route Library needs. It must be reviewed by a
qualified lawyer (AU consumer law + the jurisdictions sales reach) **before
Phase B** (before any money changes hands), per the decision in
[route-library.md](route-library.md) §12.

These three documents will surface in-product:
- **Content Policy** — what may be published (shown at submission).
- **Safety Disclaimer** — shown before download/use, and embedded in files.
- **Author Terms** — accepted on becoming an author / at each submission.

---

## 1. Content Policy (for authors)

Routes published to the Route Library **must**:
- Use only **legally accessible** ways — public roads, public land, tracks
  open to the relevant activity, or land where the author has permission.
- Respect access rules — seasonal closures, permits, private-property
  boundaries, Indigenous/protected land, national-park vehicle rules.
- Be **accurate** to the author's genuine recording, not fabricated.
- Carry honest metadata (distance, difficulty, surface, activity).

Routes **must not**:
- Cross private land without permission, or encourage trespass.
- Promote illegal activity (e.g. unregistered-vehicle use where prohibited,
  off-track riding in exclusion zones, speed/competition on public roads).
- Endanger sensitive environments or cultural-heritage sites.
- Contain personal data of third parties, or infringe others' copyright
  (someone else's roadbook/route).
- Be duplicated/spam or deliberately misleading.

Enforcement: listings violating this policy are rejected at curation or
unpublished after the fact; repeat violations → author status `paused` /
`removed`. RouteMapper may remove any listing at its discretion.

## 2. Safety Disclaimer (for users)

Shown prominently before first download and embedded as `LICENSE.txt` /
`DISCLAIMER.txt` in every downloaded package:

> Routes in the RouteMapper Route Library are user-contributed records of a
> past journey. They are provided **"as is", for general guidance only**, and
> may be inaccurate, out of date, or unsafe. Conditions change — weather,
> closures, terrain, access permissions, and hazards. **You are solely
> responsible** for assessing whether a route is safe, legal, and suitable
> for you, your vehicle/equipment, and the current conditions, and for
> navigating safely. Do not operate a device or read a roadbook in a way that
> distracts from safe control of your vehicle. Carry appropriate maps,
> communications, and emergency equipment. RouteMapper and the route author
> accept **no liability** for any loss, injury, or damage arising from use of
> a route, to the extent permitted by law.

(AU note: this cannot exclude non-excludable Australian Consumer Law
guarantees — the final wording must be lawyer-reviewed so the limitation is
valid and not misleading.)

## 3. Author Terms (accepted on becoming an author and at each submission)

By submitting a route, the author represents and agrees that:
- **Ownership/rights:** they created the route record themselves (or have the
  right to publish and sell it) and it doesn't infringe anyone's rights.
- **Licence to RouteMapper:** they grant RouteMapper a non-exclusive,
  worldwide licence to host, display, distribute and (in Phase B+) sell the
  route via the Library, and to generate previews/derived metadata.
- **Buyer licence:** buyers/downloaders receive a personal-use licence — no
  resale or redistribution.
- **Accuracy & policy:** the listing complies with the Content Policy (§1) and
  metadata is honest.
- **Revenue share (Phase B+):** RouteMapper retains **40%**; the author
  receives **60%** of the net sale price (after payment-processor fees/taxes;
  exact handling defined at Phase B). Payouts initially manual; via Stripe
  Connect from Phase D.
- **Eligibility:** authorship requires a current RouteMapper subscription and
  active author status, and may be revoked for policy breaches.
- **Removal:** RouteMapper may remove listings or suspend authorship for
  policy/legal reasons.
