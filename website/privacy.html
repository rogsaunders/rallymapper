Privacy Policy

Route Mapper respects your privacy. We collect only the information needed to provide the Route Mapper service and to communicate with supporters and customers.

Information We Collect

  • Account details you provide on sign-up: email address, full name, optional phone number, and a public username
  • Billing details handled by Stripe when you subscribe (we never see or store your card data)
  • Stages and waypoints you choose to save to the cloud
  • App crash reports (anonymised)
  • Non-identifying usage analytics to improve features

What We Do Not Collect

  • No live location data is sent to our servers unless you save or export a route yourself
  • No advertising tracking
  • No data sold or shared with third parties

How We Use Collected Data

To operate the RouteMapper service, sync your stages across your devices, process subscription payments via Stripe, and notify you of important account or release updates. Phone numbers, when provided, are used for two-factor authentication, in-vehicle emergency contact, and support — never marketing.

Contact
