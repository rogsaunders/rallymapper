Frequently Asked Questions

Does it work offline?

Yes, completely. All data is stored locally on your device and syncs automatically when a connection returns. Guest Mode requires no login and works entirely offline.

What export formats are supported?

Route Mapper exports to Rally Navigator, Hema Maps, Guru Maps, and Google Earth. Supported formats include GPX, KML, and JSON for maximum compatibility with navigation systems.

What devices does it work on?

Currently iPad-native (iPadOS 14+), optimised for both landscape and portrait orientations. Works on iPad, iPad Air, iPad Pro, and iPad mini. iPhone and Android versions are planned for future releases.

When is the Android version coming?

Android development is planned for v4.2, subject to demand and funding from this campaign. We'll prioritize based on backer feedback and interest.

Can I use this for hiking or mountain biking?

Absolutely! While designed for rally navigation, Route Mapper works perfectly for any route planning activity including hiking, mountain biking, overlanding, and expedition planning.

Is there a free trial?

Early backers will receive priority access to the TestFlight beta. After official launch, we'll offer a free Guest Mode with core features, plus a premium subscription for advanced capabilities and cloud sync.
