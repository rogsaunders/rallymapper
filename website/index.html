Frequently Asked Questions

Does it work offline?

Yes, completely. All data is stored locally on your device and syncs automatically when a connection returns. Guest Mode requires no login and works entirely offline.

What export formats are supported?

RouteMapper produces 10+ formats in a single ZIP: Rally Navigator CSV (raw and driver modes), Garmin GPX, OpenRally-compatible GPX, Universal GPX, Combined GPX, Google Earth KML, Hema Maps, Guru Maps, Gaia GPS, Roadbook HTML with tulip diagrams, and a complete Master JSON. All from one tap.

What devices does it work on?

RouteMapper is optimised for iPad (iPadOS 14+) — iPad, iPad Air, iPad Pro, and iPad mini — in both landscape and portrait orientations. The PWA runs on iPhone too, but the interface is designed for tablet real estate. Android tablet support is on the roadmap post-launch.

When is the Android version coming?

Android tablet support is planned post-App Store launch. We'll prioritise based on demand and field feedback from the iOS release.

Can I use this for hiking or mountain biking?

Absolutely! While designed for rally navigation, Route Mapper works perfectly for any route planning activity including hiking, mountain biking, overlanding, and expedition planning.

Is there a free trial?

Request early access and you'll receive a TestFlight invitation when beta opens. Guest Mode is available now for instant offline use with no login required. Post-launch pricing details will be announced closer to the App Store release.
